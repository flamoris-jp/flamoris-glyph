FLAMORIS modifies the sampled centerlines (proportions, slant, bow, corner
smoothing, terminal extension) and constructs new geometric ink contours.

The lyric was written collaboratively in this conversation. It is kept in
`lyrics.txt`, unchanged across all visual performances. No audio or recording
is included, and no singing alignment was inferred.
